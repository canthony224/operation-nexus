# SPEC: Fog of War

Status: Approved (owner, 2026-10-19) · Tracker: NX-150…NX-154 · Inputs: P228–238; owner rule C22 ("AI should only know what it can see")

## 1. Tiers (coalition perspective)
| Tier | Condition | Shown to viewers |
|---|---|---|
| 0 Unknown | No coverage, no SIGINT | Nothing |
| 1 SIGINT | Group of enemy units outside sensors that is **moving** (mobile units with speed > 0 at the refresh tick; facilities never form blobs) and within `fow.sigint_range_mult` × the range of a friendly radar site (Q-FOW-7; parked, silent units leave no blob), aggregated per **cluster** (grid cell, ≥1 unit). The same rule builds the director's view | Fuzzy blob drawn at its 10 km cell (no server jitter; the overlay may add a cosmetic offset derived from the blob id, Q-FOW-5), refreshed every ~48–60 s of world time (PROPOSED; about 8–10 s real at `sim.time_scale` 6, Q-ALN-23, Q-ALN-38), size bucketed (S/M/L), id `<refresh_tick>-<cell_index>` (FOW-2) |
| 2 Track | Inside any coalition sensor (unit, radar site). **Deterministic (Q-FOW-2):** a target is tracked when it is inside the sensor's range (`sensor.radius_m`; per-domain or per-band ranges later), times modifiers (stealth; jamming later; terrain masking); no detection roll. **Terrain masking (Q-FOW-3):** NOE/LOW aircraft and ground units inside a theater `masking_regions` polygon (rasterized to grid cells at load) are hidden from sensors outside that region; no line-of-sight rays | Exact symbol, type, heading, health bar. A tracked target may also carry the **painted** flag (recon unit painting within paint range): a marker and a combat bonus, not a separate tier (Q-FOW-6) |

## 2. Requirements
- **FOW-1** Redaction MUST happen server-side, before serialization. The public predicate is "currently tracked by the coalition"; an entity in the coalition's last-known set may appear only in `LastKnownView` (FOW-6a). Events use "currently tracked" only, so a last-known shooter stays unnamed. Hidden entities MUST NOT appear in any payload sent to the overlay, the companion site, the public-site exports pushed while a campaign is running (leaderboards/stats JSON, Q-ARCH-16), or replays meant for the public.
- **FOW-1a** **After the campaign ends (Q-ALN-21):** FoW applies only during the live campaign. Once a campaign has ended, its full archive (Q-ARCH-16), its replays and recaps, and the AAR "what you didn't see" reveal are exempt from FOW-1 and may show full, unredacted history. Anything published while the campaign is still running stays under FOW-1.
- **FOW-2** SIGINT blobs MUST NOT leak entity ids, exact counts, or exact positions. Positions are the cell only; there is no server jitter and no `fow` RNG stream (Q-FOW-5).
- **FOW-3** Events (kill feed, tracers) involving hidden enemy units MUST be redacted ("Unknown contact destroyed VIPER-27").
- **FOW-4** **Director fairness:** the Threat Director MUST decide using the *enemy-side* FoW view, built with the same algorithm from the enemy's sensors. This includes the jobs it posts on the Static's Tasking Board (Q-GD-34, THREAT-DIRECTOR TD-2). Exception: spawning in its own territory.
- **FOW-5** FoW computation uses the spatial grid and runs every `fow.track_every_ticks` (PROPOSED 5 = 0.5 s; every tick if the NX-345 spike shows it is cheap; Q-FOW-6) for Tier 2 and the painted flag; combat always uses the latest computed view; Tier 2 runs on ticks where `tick % fow.track_every_ticks == 0`, and the computed view, last-known tracks, emission flags painted/spotted flags and each side's discovered facility markers (FOW-13) are world state, included in the snapshot and `state_hash`; and every ~48–60 s of world time for Tier 1 (the sensor refresh; PROPOSED until harness-validated, chosen so the real cadence stays about 8–10 s at `sim.time_scale` 6; Q-ALN-23, Q-ALN-38).
- **FOW-8** **Per-side Tasking Boards (Q-GD-37 item 9):** each side's board is generated only from that side's own FoW view. A coalition job MUST NOT reference an entity the coalition can't see, except a discovered facility marker: a strike job may target a marker, but the striker still needs a track at impact (Q-FOW-4, Q-FOW-16); a scout job against a SIGINT blob (Q-FOW-4) references the blob's opaque id and quantized cell, never an entity id. The overlay's NEEDED list is therefore redacted by construction (FOW-1).
- **FOW-9** **Time and pauses (Q-CFG-8, Q-ALN-23):** every FoW timer (Tier 1 refresh, `fow.track_fade_s`) is **world time** (scaled by `sim.time_scale`, SIM-CORE SC-2) and is converted to ticks at config load, rounding up (Q-ALN-6). While the campaign is paused (SIM-CORE SC-11) none advances: a last-known track does not age and no blob refreshes.
- **FOW-10** **Enemy knowledge of nodes (Q-SIM-10, Q-GP-27):** both sides' views always include node (base) positions and owners. Enemy facilities are known only once discovered (FOW-13). `EnemyView` always includes node positions, the state of the enemy's own nodes and the owner of every node, and every defended node's defense HP (public node state, Q-SIM-75: combat targets it without a track, COMBAT CB-10; `SideView.defense_hp`). It never includes coalition stock or coalition unit positions except through current enemy detections, last-known tracks and discovered facility markers (FOW-13). The director may scout (ordinary ENEMY units, Q-SIM-11) to refresh them.

- **FOW-11** **Emitters (Q-FOW-8, revised by Q-FOW-12/15):** the emitting kinds are RADAR_SITE and the SAM variant of AIR_DEFENSE_SITE; every other facility has `emitting: null`. An emission is detected only by a passive sensor looking for it (FOW-13); with no passive sensor in range it reveals nothing. A dark site cannot be detected passively. **A dark site provides no sensor coverage and no SIGINT range,** so going dark always costs something. **Control (Q-FOW-10):** every site emits by default. The Static's director turns its sites dark through its `DecoyBlink` operation (automated, deterministic, from its own view; Pattern of Life radar cycles also run through DecoyBlink; how long a site stays dark is `director.decoy_blink.dark_s`). Coalition sites stay on in the first build: no player command and no AI logic. **Asymmetry (Q-FOW-13, GAMEPLAY §0):** only the Static can blink in the first build; it is a deception tool for the AI enemy, and coalition parity arrives with SEAD, when going dark has a reason. A self-protection rule (go dark when an anti-radiation missile is detected inbound, resume after a cooldown) and a possible rank-gated player command come with SEAD (GAMEPLAY-LATER). **A Disabled facility does nothing until repaired (GAMEPLAY §5b, Q-SIM-66):** it senses nothing (Tier 2), adds no SIGINT range, does not emit (it is not heard) and does not listen passively, as if dark; it is still tracked and discovered like any site, and a Disabled non-facility unit keeps its sensors. In code since NX-124 (`fog.is_off_facility`). A Disabled emitter's `FacilityMarker.emitting` is `false` (the entity keeps its own flag for when it is repaired).
- **FOW-12** **Spotted (Q-FOW-9):** each of a side's own units carries `spotted: true` while any enemy sensor tracks it. It reveals only that something sees the unit, never what or where.

- **FOW-13** **Facility discovery (Q-FOW-12, Q-FOW-15):** a side knows its own facilities. It knows an enemy facility only after discovering it: one of its sensors tracks the site, a **passive** sensor (recon units and radar sites in the first build; AWACS later) is within `sensor.passive_range_m` of the site while it emits, or the side captures the site's base. Discovery gives a **permanent marker** for that side for the rest of the campaign: position, kind, variant and engagement ring; it never fades. **In code (NX-124 part 3):** the ring is `ring_m`, the longest AIR weapon range of the site's variant (null for a site with no AIR weapon), next to the damage `state`; both are as of the last observing pass. The marker's details (damage state, emitting or dark, later upgrades) refresh only while the side observes the site again (active track, or passive detection while it emits) and otherwise show the last confirmed values with their age. A strike, repair or change the side did not sense is never revealed. The director discovers coalition facilities the same way. When a side loses a base, it keeps markers for that base's facilities with their state as of the flip tick. **In code (NX-125):** at a base flip the new owner drops its contact and marker of each facility (they are its own now) and the side that lost the base gets a marker with the site's kind, variant, position and emitting flag as of the flip tick (`fog.hand_over`); "discovers the captured base's facilities" is therefore this hand-over, since a facility always belongs to its base's owner (TH-10). `SideView.owners` carries every node's live owner (FOW-10). Facilities never produce last-known tracks; the marker replaces them.

## 2a. Track fade (owner, 2026-10-17)
- **FOW-6a (first build, both sides; Q-FOW-1, partly reverses Q-ALN-20):** when sensor coverage of a tracked contact is lost (scout destroyed, out of range, radar jammed), the contact does not vanish. It becomes a **last-known track**: its position, kind and heading freeze and are shown with an age, and after `fow.track_fade_s` (PROPOSED, world time) it is dropped. **Its lifetime never depends on whether the entity still exists:** it is removed only when the fade time elapses or the side tracks the entity again, so a hidden death or despawn is never revealed early. Last-known tracks are never valid targets. The director's `EnemyView` uses the same last-known tracks (FOW-10, THREAT-DIRECTOR TD-5).
- **FOW-6** (later phase, behind a feature flag; Q-PROD-8, Q-ALN-20; NX-301 does not block P1): the last-known track additionally grows an uncertainty circle and decays into a SIGINT blob instead of being dropped.

## 2b. Target handoff (P4)
- **FOW-7:** A painting scout can hand a target to a long-range shooter (strike missile, artillery, theater asset). That extends the shooter's effective range (beyond its own sensors) and raises Pk while the paint holds. If the paint drops, the shot reverts to its normal track-quality rule; with no track it fizzles.

## 3. Tests
- Property: no redacted payload contains an entity that fails the visibility predicate.
- Blob ids contain no EntityId, every blob sits at a cell centre, and building blobs draws no RNG (Q-FOW-5).
- Director decision functions receive only `EnemyView` (enforced by types).
- FOW-8: property: no job on either side's board references an entity that fails that side's visibility predicate.
- FOW-9: a last-known track's age (and, with FOW-6 on, its uncertainty radius) is identical at the pause tick and the resume tick; with `sim.time_scale` = 6 the Tier 1 refresh fires after one sixth of the ticks it takes at `sim.time_scale` = 1 (before tick rounding).
- FOW-1: a public-site export built mid-campaign contains no entity that fails the coalition visibility predicate.
- FOW-1a: the end-of-campaign archive and "what you didn't see" view are built only after the campaign has ended; attempting to build either for a running campaign is rejected.
- FOW-6a: losing coverage produces a last-known track with the frozen position and an age; it is dropped after `fow.track_fade_s`, and the same holds in `EnemyView`.
- Q-FOW-2: detection is a pure function of positions, ranges and modifiers (no RNG draw); Q-FOW-3: a LOW aircraft inside a masking region is not tracked by a radar outside it. (In code since NX-124 part 3, `tests/core/test_fog.py`: NOE and LOW aircraft are masked like ground units; MED and HIGH never.)
- Q-FOW-4: no unit engages a target that is not tracked, including last-known tracks.
- Q-FOW-7: a parked group makes no blob, and a moving group beyond `fow.sigint_range_mult` × radar radius makes no blob, in both views.
- FOW-11/FOW-13: an undiscovered enemy facility appears in no payload; an emitting site within a recon unit's or radar site's passive range is discovered and shows `emitting: true`; a dark site is never discovered passively; a dark site adds no coverage or SIGINT range.
- FOW-13: a discovered marker never fades; a strike or repair on a site the side is not observing leaves its marker's `state` and `confirmed_tick` unchanged; capturing a base discovers its facilities.
- FOW-12: `spotted` is true exactly while an enemy sensor tracks the unit, and null on other-side entities.
- Q-FOW-10: no coalition site goes dark in the first build; DecoyBlink is chosen only from `EnemyView`.
- Q-FOW-6: between FoW passes combat uses the last computed view, and a snapshot restore between passes replays to the same `state_hash`.
- FOW-6a: a hidden destruction or despawn never shortens a last-known track's life.
