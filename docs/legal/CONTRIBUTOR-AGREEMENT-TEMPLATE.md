# Contributor Agreement (template)

Status: **DRAFT v0, not for use** · Tracker: NX-347 · Decisions: Q-LEGAL-13 · Related: NX-289 (human voice lines), NX-319 (art plan), NX-348 (credits page)

> **This is a template written by a software assistant, not legal advice.** The owner decided the principles in Q-LEGAL-13 (2026-10-18); the wording is not reviewed by a lawyer. One narrow attorney review of this template is required **before the first paid use** (Q-LEGAL-13). Items in [BRACKETS] are blanks to fill per contributor. Items marked **PROPOSED** are drafting choices that still need the owner's answer (see the table at the end). Nothing here is a promise to anyone until a signed copy exists. Signed copies and contributor details are kept in the private vault, never in this public repo.

## What this covers
One contributor, one agreed piece of work (art, voice lines, music, writing). The same text is used each time; only the blanks change.

## 3. Fee
- **Flat fee:** [amount] USD for the Work, set before work starts.
- **Paid:** [PROPOSED: within 14 days after the Project accepts the Work] by [payment method, to be agreed].
- **No royalties or revenue share.** The fee is the whole payment. A bonus exists only if both sides sign a written addendum that says so.
- **Taxes:** the Contributor is responsible for their own taxes. The Project may ask for the details it needs to pay and report [ATTORNEY/ACCOUNTANT: confirm what applies].
- **If the Project cancels** after work has started, it pays for the work done so far [PROPOSED: pro rata].

## 4. Who owns the Work
The Contributor keeps the copyright in the Work. The Project receives the licence in §5, nothing more.

## 5. Licence to the Project
The Contributor grants the Project a worldwide licence, [PROPOSED: perpetual], [PROPOSED: exclusive for the named character or asset, non-exclusive otherwise; see D2], to copy, edit, mix, adapt, stream, publish and display the Work as part of Operation Nexus, meaning:
- the game, the live stream and its recordings, clips, replays and highlights;
- the Project's website, trailers, devlogs and marketing for the Project;
- on any platform the Project uses to host or distribute those things, and to the services that host or distribute them for the Project.

Editing means ordinary production work (trimming, mixing, levelling, resizing). It does not mean making the Contributor appear to say or do something they did not record or create.

The Contributor may show the Work in their own portfolio after it is released [PROPOSED].

## 6. No AI training and no voice cloning
The Project will not, and will not allow anyone else to, use the Work or any recording of the Contributor to train, fine-tune or evaluate any AI or machine-learning model, or to create a synthetic copy of the Contributor's voice, face or style. Any exception needs a separate signed agreement and separate payment. The Project's own use of AI voices and tools is separate and does not use the Work.

## 7. Credit
- The Project credits the Contributor by their credit name on its credits page, in the stream's about section, and in the descriptions of videos that use the Work.
- If the credit is missed, the Project fixes it within [PROPOSED: 14 days] of being told.
- **Removal:** on request the Project removes the Contributor's name from **new** content and from the credits page. Content already published stays as it is, except that the Project will remove the name from descriptions where it practically can [PROPOSED]. The licence in §5 does not end because credit was removed.

## 8. What the Contributor promises
- The Work is the Contributor's own and original, and the Contributor has the right to licence it.
- It contains no third-party material (samples, stock, fonts, voices, likenesses) unless listed in writing with its licence.
- **AI-generated content:** [PROPOSED: the Work contains none; the Contributor does not use generative AI to produce it]. If the Contributor wants to use AI tools, they ask first and the tool is named in writing.
- The Contributor is at least 18 years old [PROPOSED].
- The Work does not use a real person's name, likeness or voice without permission, and follows the Project's content rules (a fictional world; no real people, organisations or brands; no hateful content).

## 9. Confidentiality
The Contributor may see unreleased story material. They keep it confidential until the Project publishes it, and do not share, post or leak it. This does not cover information that was already public or that the Contributor already had. [ATTORNEY: confirm wording and duration.]

## 10. Ending the agreement
- Either side may stop the engagement in writing before the Work is delivered; §3 covers payment for work done.
- If the Project has not paid an accepted invoice [PROPOSED: 30 days] after being reminded in writing, the licence for **that unpaid Work** is suspended until it is paid.
- Otherwise the licence in §5 continues for Work that has been paid for.

## 11. Personal information
The Project keeps the Contributor's name, contact and payment details only to run this agreement and meet legal duties. The only thing published is the credit name. The Project's privacy contact is [project address on the project domain, per Q-LEGAL-6]. [ATTORNEY: privacy wording.]

## 12. Independent contractor
