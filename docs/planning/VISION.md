# Vision — Operation Nexus

## One sentence
Live-stream chat plays a 24/7 co-op war against an adaptive AI on a sleek command-center map. Anyone can join in one command, and the frontline changes over days.

## Why it can work
- **Not AI slop:** the experience rests on deterministic rules, persistent state and real consequences (logistics, supply lines, fog of war), not generated filler.
- **Zero-friction entry, deep mastery:** `!join strike` puts you on screen immediately. Veterans unlock force-multiplier roles that keep newcomers alive.
- **Always alive:** the AI garrison and Threat Director keep the world moving at 3 a.m., and a lone night-owl can be the hero.

## Pillars
1. **Readable in 5 seconds:** momentum bar, current objective, threat level.
2. **Every viewer matters:** a guaranteed personal unit, instant visual acknowledgement, and the spotlight on your unit.
3. **Logistics is heroism:** supply decides the war, and the logistics specialist is the one everyone needs.
4. **A fair, adaptive enemy:** it sees only what it can sense, and it learns from what you do, visibly.
5. **Calm by default:** an ambient war room you can leave on all night.

## Audience
Mil-sim and strategy fans (DEFCON, Foxhole, HighFleet, Wargame), casual lurkers who want to take part, and the devlog audience.

## Out of scope for v1
PvP between viewers, TikTok chat control, 3D views, voice cloning, a global multi-theater map, paid power.

## Success (first 6 months; confirm Q-PROD-1)
- Closed alpha: 7 days unattended, and testers understand the stream within 5 s.
- Public beta: median session ≥ 12 min, ≥ 20% of viewers issue a command, D7 returning commanders ≥ 15%.
- Zero moderation incidents on stream.
