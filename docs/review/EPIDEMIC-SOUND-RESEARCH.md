## Implications
1. **Before relying on it, get written confirmation from Epidemic Sound support** that a continuous, unattended, *monetized* 24/7 game stream is covered. The Creator plan is described as non-commercial, so a monetized channel may need **Pro or Business**. [VERIFIED 2026-10-18: the Creator plan is called non-commercial yet allows monetizing one channel per platform (LICENSE-AND-MUSIC-VERIFICATION-2026-10-18 V-80); the Pro and Business plans do not cover Video on Demand (VOD), Streaming VOD or Pay-per-View (LICENSE-AND-MUSIC-VERIFICATION-2026-10-18 V-86).]
2. Connect exactly one Twitch and one YouTube channel.
3. If the subscription lapses, the live stream stops being covered. Whether VODs and clips stay covered is [UNVERIFIED]. [Still UNVERIFIED on 2026-10-18: content published during the subscription stays cleared (LICENSE-AND-MUSIC-VERIFICATION-2026-10-18 V-81), but whether a continuing broadcast counts as published, and whether an archived livestream counts as VOD, is not addressed by any primary page (V-82, V-86).]
4. Music agent: use the **official MCP server** with a personal key, kept in the secret store. Put it behind a `MusicSource` adapter (ADR-0010), and keep it **off the hot path**: an agent curates playlists ahead of time, and playback stays deterministic.
