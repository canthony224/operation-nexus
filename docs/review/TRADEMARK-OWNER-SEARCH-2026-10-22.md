## Reading
- **No HIVEPLEX mark was found** in the US, EU, UK or WIPO databases, filed or pending, in the screenshots.
- **Hiveplex LTD** is a UK company the owner says sells bike parts. Its only mark seen is NEONIX, not HIVEPLEX. A company name is not a trademark. A UK business trading as Hiveplex could still have unregistered rights for bike parts in the UK (and in the US if it sells there). Nothing here shows that.
- **Goods differ.** Bike parts against a livestreamed game and community (entertainment and software) are far apart, and the owner is US-based and the project is a stream, not a sold game. That lowers confusion risk, but it does not remove it for an identical name.
- **The UKIPO neighbours** (HIVEXCEL, Hemiplex, HELIPLEX, VIEWPLEX) are different words in unrelated goods. Nothing in the screenshots makes them a blocker.
- **Bigger question, unchanged from the earlier research:** the PLEX ending against Plex, Inc. (media software and entertainment). The screenshots do not cover it. See TRADEMARK-AND-NAME-VERIFICATION-2026-10-18.

## Not covered by these searches
- `HIVE PLEX` with a space, `PLEX` alone, and design marks.
- TMview (national offices beyond the EU), US state registers and unregistered use (web search).
- Whether Hiveplex LTD sells in the US or uses the name beyond bike parts.
- Classes 9, 28, 35, 38, 41 and 42 for the same word.
